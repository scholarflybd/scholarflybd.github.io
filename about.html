ABOUT SCHOLARFLY BD

Guidance that starts with your goals

ScholarFly BD is an independent student support initiative helping applicants navigate international scholarship and research opportunities.

OUR MISSION

Make international education easier to understand

Scholarship information is often scattered across universities, government portals, and research groups. Our aim is to help students organize that information and approach applications strategically.

  • Focus on official eligibility requirements and deadlines
  • Support applicants across different academic disciplines
  • Explain government, university, and research funding clearly
  • Protect applicants' personal documents and data

Our guiding principles

Clarity, accessibility, transparency, and realistic expectations. We do not promise an admission, scholarship, supervisor response, or visa.

Our global catalogue will grow as programmes are researched and verified.
