OUR STORY

International study should feel more understandable.

ScholarFly BD exists to help students make informed decisions about scholarships, university applications and funded academic opportunities.

What we're here to do

Scholarship advice can be difficult to navigate. Requirements change, funding routes differ, and students do not always know where to begin.

We bring essential information together in one place and offer practical guidance tailored to a student's goals. We value careful research, transparent communication and realistic expectations.

Our work spans government awards, institutional scholarships, graduate assistantships, research groups and supervisor-led funding.
