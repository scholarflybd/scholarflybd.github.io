SCHOLARFLY BD

Clear guidance. Thoughtful preparation.

ScholarFly BD is an independent educational guidance initiative helping students and researchers explore international study and funding opportunities.

WHO WE ARE

A practical starting point for global study

Choosing where to study can mean navigating unfamiliar requirements, funding routes and application systems. ScholarFly BD helps applicants organise that information and turn their goals into a practical application plan.

We support students, graduates and researchers, including Bangladeshi applicants exploring opportunities abroad. Our guidance begins with your qualifications, interests and circumstances.

Our purpose

Make international education information clearer, more accessible and more useful, so applicants can make informed decisions and prepare with confidence.

Our independence

We do not represent universities, governments or scholarship providers. Admission, funding and visa decisions are made by the relevant institutions.

What we help you work through

Support for each stage, from understanding your profile to preparing a focused application.

Understand your starting point

Review education, grades, research experience, language preparation and academic goals.

Explore suitable routes

Compare countries, universities, scholarship programmes and research funding pathways.

Prepare with purpose

Plan an academic CV, statement of purpose, research proposal, supervisor enquiry and interview preparation.

Who we support

  • Prospective undergraduate and Master's students
  • Integrated MS/PhD and doctoral applicants
  • Postdoctoral researchers seeking their next position
  • Graduates and professionals returning to study

You can start even if you have not chosen a country, completed an English test or published a paper.

What you can expect

  • Advice connected to your own academic profile
  • Clear distinctions between confirmed facts and information to check
  • Respectful handling of your submitted information
  • Any paid scope and fee explained before work begins

We do not promise admission, funding, supervisor replies or visas. Strong preparation helps you apply thoughtfully; it cannot determine an institution's decision.
