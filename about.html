About ScholarFly BD

An independent initiative helping students and researchers explore global opportunities.

Who we are

ScholarFly BD is an independent international education guidance initiative. We help students and researchers explore global opportunities, understand scholarship requirements and improve their application preparation. We have no official affiliation with any university, government or funding body.

Our mission

To make international study and funding easier to understand, so applicants spend their effort on the right opportunities.

Our vision

A world where a strong academic profile, not access to scattered information, decides who gets to study abroad.

What we do

We assess academic profiles, match applicants with relevant funding routes, support university and supervisor searches, and help with CVs, statements of purpose, research proposals and application documents.

Our approach

We work from official sources, say clearly when information is unverified, and give individual feedback rather than generic advice. We do not promise outcomes.

Who we support

Bachelor's, Master's, PhD and postdoctoral applicants, including early-career researchers and working professionals.

Why students choose ScholarFly BD

Transparent information, student-centred support and realistic expectations.
