About ScholarFly BD

An independent initiative helping students and researchers explore global opportunities.

Who we are

ScholarFly BD is an independent educational guidance initiative for students, graduates and researchers exploring study opportunities abroad. We help applicants make sense of scholarships, university admission routes and research funding so they can plan with greater confidence. Our guidance is independent: we do not represent or speak for universities, governments or funding organisations.

Our mission

To make international education information clearer, more accessible and more useful. We aim to help each applicant identify suitable programmes, understand requirements and prepare applications with care rather than sending the same advice to everyone.

Our vision

We believe promising students should be able to explore international education regardless of how much guidance they already have. We want to make credible information and thoughtful preparation easier to access.

What we help with

Our support covers initial academic profile evaluation, country and university shortlisting, government and university scholarships, professor-funded research opportunities, and application preparation. Depending on your goals, we can help you plan an academic CV, statement of purpose, research proposal, supervisor enquiry, document checklist or scholarship interview preparation.

How we work

Every applicant has a different starting point. We first consider qualifications, grades, research experience, language proficiency, intended field, preferred countries and likely funding routes. We then suggest practical next steps and highlight requirements that need further checking. Scholarship dates, conditions and benefits are confirmed against the awarding institution whenever possible; unverified information is labelled clearly.

Who we support

We welcome prospective Bachelor's and Master's students, doctoral applicants, postdoctoral researchers, recent graduates and professionals returning to study. Applicants without publications, test scores or a final country preference can still request an initial assessment.

What you can expect

Clear communication, realistic guidance and a respectful review of your academic plans. We never guarantee admission, scholarship funding, supervisor responses or visa approval. If a service involves a fee, its scope and cost should be explained before you decide to proceed.

Your next step

Start with our academic profile assessment to share your education history and study goals. This helps us understand your situation before recommending possible destinations and funding pathways.

Our team

Team profiles will be published here once names, photographs and qualifications are confirmed.

Not sure where to begin?

Share your academic background and study goals, and we will help you map out realistic next steps.
