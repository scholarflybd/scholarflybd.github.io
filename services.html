Profile assessment

Understand your academic strengths, missing information and next steps.

  • Research, work and language preparation
  • Study goals and funding preferences

02 / EXPLORE

University & scholarship planning

Build a focused shortlist and check requirements at their source.

  • Programme and destination comparison
  • Eligibility and document checklist
  • Application timeline planning

03 / RESEARCH

Supervisor search

Identify research groups relevant to your interests and prepare a specific enquiry.

  • Research fit and recent work
  • Advertised positions and funding routes
  • Personalised outreach guidance

Academic CV feedback

Present your experience in a clear, accurate and relevant way.

  • Structure and readability
  • Research, projects and achievements
  • Consistency with your application

05 / EXPLAIN YOUR FIT

SOP & proposal feedback

Strengthen the logic and organisation of your own writing.

  • Motivation and programme fit
  • Research question and feasibility
  • Clarity, evidence and academic integrity

06 / GET READY

Interview preparation

Practise explaining your background, research and goals with confidence.

  • Academic introduction
  • Project and research discussion
  • Questions for the programme or lab
