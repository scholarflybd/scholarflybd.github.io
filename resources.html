Prepare one clear step at a time.

Practical planning guides for international study and research applications. Always follow the specific instructions published by your chosen institution.

UNDERGRADUATE

Bachelor's applications

Begin with your school qualifications and intended subject.

  • Check whether your qualifications meet entry requirements.
  • Record subject prerequisites, required tests and grading scales.
  • Compare tuition, living costs and the actual scope of funding.
  • Prepare school records and any required references or essays.

POSTGRADUATE

Master's applications

Decide whether a taught course or research programme better serves your goals.

  • Check your degree subject and academic eligibility.
  • Compare course content, thesis opportunities and career relevance.
  • Prepare a focused statement of purpose and academic CV.
  • For research programmes, check supervisor and funding requirements.

RESEARCH DEGREES

PhD and integrated MS/PhD

Show a clear connection between your preparation and the proposed research.

  • Read the position or programme's entry requirements.
  • Review relevant lab publications and current research themes.
  • Explain your previous projects and your contribution accurately.
  • Check funding duration, tuition coverage and application routes.
  • Develop a proposal only in the format requested by the institution.

AFTER THE PHD

Postdoctoral opportunities

Present the research contribution and methods you can bring to a group.

  • Match your expertise to the advertised project or fellowship.
  • Prepare a research CV, publication list and concise statement of fit.
  • Check eligibility windows and host requirements for each fellowship.
  • Discuss the research plan, supervision, contract and funding terms.

Your application document checklist

Requirements differ. Use this as a planning list, then follow the official checklist.

Academic record

  • Degree certificates or expected completion letter
  • Transcripts with the grading scale
  • Translations or certified copies only where required
  • Language or admissions test evidence, where required

Your academic story

  • Accurate CV with dates and relevant experience
  • Statement of purpose tailored to the programme
  • Research summary or proposal where requested
  • Publication links and a clear description of your contribution

References & organisation

  • Referees who know your work, contacted in advance
  • A tracker for deadlines, time zones and submission routes
  • Consistent names and dates across documents
  • Readable PDFs with clear filenames

Write a focused supervisor enquiry

  1. Introduce your degree, institution and intended programme.
  2. Refer to a specific research theme or publication you have read.
  3. Explain how your experience connects to the group's work.
  4. Ask a clear question about suitable opportunities or the application route.
  5. Attach a concise CV if appropriate and follow any published contact instructions.

Before you send

Check the professor's name, institution, research fit and email address. Keep the message specific and truthful. A reply is not an admission or funding offer.

Before you submit

Check the official deadline and time zone, preview uploaded documents, confirm reference arrangements and save your submission receipt.
