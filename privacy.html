Privacy Notice

How ScholarFly BD handles information you submit for an academic profile assessment. Updated 24 October 2026.

Information you choose to provide

The assessment form requests your name, email, nationality, country of residence, qualifications, study goals and funding preferences. You may also provide a WhatsApp number, academic results, test scores, research and work experience, publication links and an optional CV sharing link.

Purpose

We use this information to review your academic profile and contact you about your enquiry. Submitting the form does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

Where responses go

The form is hosted by Google Forms and linked to a private Google Sheet owned by scholarflybd@gmail.com. The account owner receives an email notification when a response is submitted. The notification links to the private Sheet rather than reproducing your answers.

Access and sharing

Your responses are not published on this website or stored in its public GitHub repository. Access is limited to the ScholarFly BD account owner and any reviewers the owner authorizes. We do not sell your submitted information. Sending your documents to a university, professor or other external recipient requires a separate arrangement with you.

Optional CV links

If you choose to provide a Google Drive CV link, give Viewer access to scholarflybd@gmail.com for that file. You can manage or revoke that access in your own Drive. Avoid including unnecessary identity, financial or sensitive personal details.

Third-party services

Google processes form submissions and may handle account or technical information under its own policies. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads resources such as fonts, flags and the embedded Google Form from external providers. Opening WhatsApp, email or official scholarship links takes you to those services.

Corrections and deletion requests

Contact scholarflybd@gmail.com from the email used in your submission to request access, correction or deletion. Assessment records are used for profile review and related follow-up; contact us if you no longer want your profile retained. You can also request that we stop contacting you.

Information we do not request

Do not submit passwords, passport or national ID copies, bank details, financial documents or sensitive medical information for an initial assessment.
