Privacy Policy

Draft for owner review. Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Through the assessment form: name, contact details, education history, test scores, research experience, study preferences and optional documents such as a CV.

How we use it

To assess your profile, contact you and prepare feedback. Optional updates about scholarships are sent only if you agree separately.

Storage and access

Responses are stored in Google Forms, Google Sheets and Google Drive, accessible only to authorised ScholarFly BD reviewers. We do not publish or sell your information.

What we do not want

Please do not submit passport or national ID copies or financial documents.

Retention

We keep assessment records only as long as needed to provide feedback and follow-up, then delete them on request or on a schedule set by the owner.

Your rights

To access, correct or delete your information, email scholarflybd@gmail.com.

This notice is a draft. The owner must confirm the retention period and review it against applicable privacy law before collecting applications.
