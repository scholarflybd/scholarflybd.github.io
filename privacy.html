PRIVACY

Privacy information

A plain-language overview of how ScholarFly BD intends to handle profile-assessment information.

Information collected

Once the assessment form is connected, we may collect details submitted by applicants such as names, contact details, education history, academic interests, test scores and optional CV files.

How information is used

Information is intended for reviewing assessment requests, communicating with applicants, and preparing guidance. It should not be published or shared with unrelated third parties without an appropriate legal basis or consent.

Storage and access

Google Forms, Google Sheets and Google Drive may be used to process and store submissions. Access should be restricted to authorized reviewers with appropriate account security.

Your choices

For inquiries about information submitted, including access, correction or deletion requests, email scholarflybd@gmail.com.

Important

This is a provisional privacy notice and must be reviewed to reflect actual data practices, retention periods, applicable privacy law and any third-party processors before collecting applications.
