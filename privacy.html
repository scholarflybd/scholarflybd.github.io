PRIVACY POLICY

Your information deserves care.

This summary explains how academic profile information is handled when you contact ScholarFly BD or submit an assessment.

Information we collect

When you submit an assessment, we may collect your name, contact details, education, exam results, research experience, study preferences and optional CV link or file.

Why we use it

We use your submission to understand your academic background, respond to your enquiry and provide scholarship or study guidance. We do not use your information to guarantee admission or funding.

Where responses go

The assessment is collected through Google Forms and stored in the associated Google Sheets/Drive account controlled by the site administrator. Google processes information according to its policies. The public website repository does not store your responses.

Access and sharing

Only authorised ScholarFly BD reviewers should access the response spreadsheet. We should not share application details with universities, professors or third parties without a suitable legal basis or permission. Do not submit passports, national IDs, payment card details or bank documents at this stage.

Your choices

Contact scholarflybd@gmail.com to ask questions about your submitted information or request correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Important note

Before collecting actual student data, the site administrator must establish a retention period, secure account access, and confirm this notice accurately reflects their processing practices and applicable privacy law.
