A little background helps us give more useful guidance. Share your academic history, research interests and preferred destinations through our student assessment form.

What we ask for

The form is organised in short sections and covers only the information needed to understand your study plans.

1
Basic details

Your name, email, WhatsApp and current location.

2
Education history

SSC/HSC, bachelor’s, master’s, PhD where relevant, year, subject and CGPA.

3
Research & experience

Thesis, publications, research skills and professional work.

4
Language & academic tests

IELTS, TOEFL, GRE or other scores, if available.

5
Study preferences

Target degree, countries, funding preferences and planned intake.

6
CV & consent

Optional document link and permission to review your information.
