Student Profile Assessment

Share your academic qualifications, research experience and study preferences. Your information helps us identify realistic scholarship and university pathways.

Information we need to understand your profile

  • Personal details and contact
  • Secondary education (SSC/O-Level and HSC/A-Level)
  • Bachelor's degree (if applicable)
  • Master's degree (if applicable)
  • PhD and research profile (if applicable)
  • Language and admission tests (only those you have)
  • Preferred degree, countries, field and intake
  • Documents: CV, transcripts, SOP (optional)
  • Goals, questions and consent

Questions that do not apply to you may be left blank; the Google Form setup is being finalized. Do not upload passports, national IDs or financial documents. CV upload through Google Forms may need a Google sign-in.

What happens next

Once the Google Form is activated, responses will be stored in a private Google Sheet. Email acknowledgement and assessment feedback require administrator setup. Your information will not be displayed publicly. Read our Privacy Policy.
