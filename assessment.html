BEFORE YOU APPLY

What we will ask

  • Personal details and contact
  • SSC / HSC or equivalent academic history
  • Bachelor’s degree, subject, year, CGPA and scale
  • Master’s degree, subject, year, CGPA and scale (if applicable)
  • Thesis, publications and research experience (if applicable)
  • IELTS / TOEFL / TOPIK / GRE scores (if applicable)
  • Preferred countries, degree and intake
  • Academic CV (optional for initial review)
  • Goals, questions and consent

Do not upload passports, national IDs, financial documents or sensitive personal records for the initial assessment. Your details should be handled only through the private Google Form, not stored in this public website.
