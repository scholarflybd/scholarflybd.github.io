Ask us a question ↗
Who can request an assessment?

Prospective Bachelor's, Master's, integrated MS/PhD and PhD applicants, postdoctoral researchers, and professionals planning further study can submit a profile.

Is the initial assessment free?

Yes. If you later choose a paid service, its scope and cost will be explained in writing before work begins.

Do you guarantee admission or funding?

No. Selection decisions rest with universities, supervisors, scholarship providers and relevant authorities.

Do I need to sign in to Google?

No. You can complete the form without a Google account. Signing in may let Google save your progress, but it is optional.

Do I need IELTS, publications or a CV?

You do not need them to request an initial assessment. Leave optional questions blank. Individual programmes set their own admissions requirements.

How do I share my CV?

The form accepts an optional sharing link. For Google Drive, give Viewer access to scholarflybd@gmail.com for the individual CV file, then paste its link. Do not make an entire folder public.

What happens after I submit?

You will see a confirmation message. ScholarFly BD receives a notification and can review your answers in a private Sheet. We may contact you about your enquiry or request clarification.

Will I receive an automatic confirmation email?

The form displays confirmation on screen. An automatic email copy to applicants is not currently enabled. Keep a note of your submission and contact us if needed.

Can I change an answer after submitting?

Email scholarflybd@gmail.com from the address you used in the form, explain the correction and include your name. You do not need to submit a second profile.

Are the listed scholarships currently open?

The directory provides starting points and official resources. It is not a list of verified open calls. Check each provider for current eligibility, dates and funding.

What should I avoid sending?

Do not send passwords, passport or national ID copies, banking information, financial documents or sensitive medical details for an initial assessment.

How can I request deletion of my profile?

Email scholarflybd@gmail.com from the address used in your submission. See our Privacy Notice for details.
