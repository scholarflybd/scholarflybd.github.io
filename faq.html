Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Do you guarantee a scholarship or admission?

No. Selection decisions rest with universities, supervisors and scholarship providers.

Is the profile assessment free?

Yes, the initial assessment is free. Any later paid service is explained in writing first.

Can I apply without IELTS?

Some programmes accept alternatives or waivers, others require a test. It depends on the programme.

Do I need a CV to submit the form?

A CV helps but is optional for the initial review.

What should I not send?

Passports, national IDs and financial documents. We do not need them for the initial assessment.

Do you help with research-funded PhD positions?

Yes, we help you search for supervisors and understand assistantship funding routes.

Are the scholarships listed currently open?

Not necessarily. Entries are pathways to research, and each needs checking on the official site.

How is my information protected?

Responses go to a private Google Form and Sheet, and are not published. See the Privacy Policy.

Not sure where to begin?

Share your academic background and study goals, and we will help you map out realistic next steps.
