FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Good to know before you apply

Can you guarantee a scholarship?

No. Universities, supervisors and funding organisations make all admission and scholarship decisions. We help students research and prepare.

Can I apply without IELTS?

Some universities and scholarship schemes offer alternatives or exemptions; others require a valid test. Always check the relevant admission rules.

Do you support research-funded Master's and PhD applications?

Yes. We can help you understand lab-based funding, research positions and supervisor outreach approaches where relevant.

Can students from any country contact you?

Yes, you can ask about study destinations worldwide. Our published destination coverage is expanding and guidance depends on available verified information.
