An independent guide to international study

ScholarFly BD is an independent education guidance initiative. We help students and researchers understand scholarship requirements, compare study destinations and prepare stronger applications. We are not a university, a government body or a scholarship provider.

Our aim is accurate information and honest advice, including when a profile needs more work before applying.

  • Transparent about what we know and what still needs verifying
  • Guidance built around your own profile
  • Bachelor's, Master's, PhD and postdoctoral applicants
  • No guarantees of admission, funding or visas

Three kinds of funding

Government scholarships

National or bilateral programmes with published criteria, such as GKS, MEXT, DAAD and Chevening.

University scholarships

Merit awards, tuition waivers and fellowships offered by individual institutions.

Professor-funded positions

Research assistantships and funded PhD roles paid from a supervisor's grant. Applications go to the professor directly.

Featured scholarship programmes

How it works

  1. Share your profileComplete the assessment form with your education, interests and goals.
  2. Initial reviewWe review your background against typical programme requirements.
  3. Receive feedbackYou receive written feedback on realistic options and gaps.
  4. Build your planShortlist programmes, supervisors and deadlines.
  5. Prepare and applyWork on your CV, SOP and application documents.

Start with a profile assessment

The assessment form asks about your education, test scores, research experience and goals. Sections that do not apply to you are skipped. You do not need every test score, and you should not send passports, national IDs or financial documents.

Common questions

Do you guarantee a scholarship?

No. Decisions rest with universities, supervisors and funders.

Can I apply without IELTS?

Some programmes accept alternatives or waivers. It depends on the programme.

Is the initial assessment free?

Yes. Any later paid service is explained in writing before you commit.
